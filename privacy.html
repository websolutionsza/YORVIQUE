What we collect

WebDrop Pro is designed to let you share files without creating an account. When you use the demo drop flow on this site, your selected files stay in your browser and are not uploaded to our servers. The demo link is illustrative and does not represent a stored production file.

When you visit the website, standard technical information may be processed by our hosting provider, such as your browser type, approximate location, device information, and request logs. We use this information to keep the site reliable and secure.

How we use it

We use technical information to operate, protect, and improve WebDrop Pro. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use the demo drop interaction to build advertising profiles.

If we introduce account-based or production file-sharing features, we will update this policy before those features collect or store additional information.

Your choices

You can browse this site without an account. You can also choose not to select a file, and the browser will keep the file interaction local to your device. Your browser settings may let you control cookies, local storage, and certain analytics technologies.

Contact

If you have a privacy question, email privacy@webdrop.pro. We aim to respond within a reasonable time.

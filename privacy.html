What we collect

YORVIQUE is designed to let you share files without creating an account. When you upload a file, the file is transmitted to our storage backend (Cloudflare R2) so that the shareable link can retrieve it from anywhere. The file is stored under a cryptographically random identifier and is only accessible to people who have the link.

When you visit the website, standard technical information may be processed by our hosting provider, such as your browser type, approximate location, device information, and request logs. We use this information to keep the site reliable and secure.

How we use it

We use technical information to operate, protect, and improve YORVIQUE. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use the upload interaction to build advertising profiles.

If we introduce account-based features in the future, we will update this policy before those features collect or store additional information.

Your choices

You can browse this site without an account. You can also choose not to upload a file. If you have uploaded a file and want it removed before its link expires, contact us with the shareable URL and we will review the request.

Your browser settings may let you control cookies, local storage, and certain analytics technologies.

Contact

If you have a privacy question, email websolutionssa0@gmail.com. We aim to respond within a reasonable time.
